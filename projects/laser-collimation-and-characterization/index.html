OPTICS / CASE STUDY 16

Laser collimation and characterization

CAD + bench measurements

Laser-assembly and measurement work for the crystal-spectroscopy platform. CAD files capture a beam-collimation assembly, while lab notes and a bench photograph document laser setup and current-modulation characterization.

SolidWorksLaser alignmentCurrent driversOptical measurementLab notebooks

My contribution

  • Modeled a collimation assembly around commercial lens components and a gain-chip reference.
  • Set up the laser, drivers, fibers, and wavemeter; recorded current-modulation and optical-intensity measurements.

Deliverables

  • Beam-collimator assembly revisions
  • September laser-setup and measurement notes
  • Bench photograph and saved measurement plot

Recorded outcome

The September 4 notebook reports a driver response of 50.1 +/- 4.7 mA/V. The saved intensity sweep also records a turnover near 230 mA for that particular measurement.

50.1 ± 4.7mA/V, notebook result

Status & scope

The driver-response value is a recorded notebook result. Completed linewidth, frequency-stability, or mode-hop-free tuning measurements are not established here.

Credit & context

This is setup, integration, and characterization work within the shared Axion crystal project.

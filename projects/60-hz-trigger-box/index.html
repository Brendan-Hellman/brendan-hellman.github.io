ELECTRONICS / CASE STUDY 03

60 Hz trigger box

Built / documented

A laboratory interface that turns a 60 Hz reference and an enable input into active-low trigger pulses. The project spans the circuit, PCB revisions, rack enclosure, connector layout, wiring, and fabrication documentation.

KiCadSolidWorksPCB layoutSolderingBNC / D-sub wiring

My contribution

  • Developed the trigger-circuit and enclosure revisions, including panel cutouts and board-to-panel connector spacing.
  • Documented the transformer, comparator, one-shot, and NAND stages, plus the box wiring and assembly sequence.

Deliverables

  • KiCad schematic, PCB, and production BOM
  • SolidWorks enclosure assemblies and waterjet DXFs
  • Circuit and box assembly guide

Recorded outcome

The documented design produces a nominal 100 microsecond low pulse per selected 60 Hz edge while enabled. The guide captures corrections to connector cutouts and an earlier silkscreen ground label.

60 HzReference repetition rate
100 µsNominal pulse width

Status & scope

Pulse width is a documented design target, not a measurement newly verified for this portfolio.

Credit & context

The design uses standard comparator, monostable, NAND-gate, and regulator building blocks.

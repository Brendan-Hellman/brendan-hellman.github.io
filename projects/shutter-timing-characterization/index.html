ANALYSIS / CASE STUDY 05

Shutter timing characterization

Measured / notebook

An oscilloscope-to-Python workflow for comparing the shutter command with an optical signal. The notebook reads opening and closing traces, plots the two channels, and records the shutter’s response timing.

PythonJupyterpandasOscilloscopeTiming analysis

My contribution

  • Processed Rigol CSV traces in a Jupyter notebook using pandas, NumPy, and Matplotlib.
  • Recorded opening and closing delays using the notebook’s stated optical-signal thresholds.

Deliverables

  • Jupyter analysis notebook
  • RigolDS0.csv and RigolDS20.csv scope exports
  • Saved opening and closing plots

Recorded outcome

The notebook reports a 9.41 ms opening delay and 1.06 ms rise time; closing is reported as an 8.05 ms delay and 1.40 ms fall time. These are notebook-reported results, with no uncertainty or repeatability study supplied.

9.41 msReported opening delay
8.05 msReported closing delay

Status & scope

Timing values are transcribed from the notebook narrative. They were not recalculated or treated as calibrated specifications.

MECHANICAL / CASE STUDY 08

PVD chamber print mockup

CAD + print preparation

A physical-scale mockup workflow for a PVD chamber assembly. The source set contains the complete CAD, a part representation, and separate left- and right-side STEP exports for downstream fabrication or printing.

SolidWorksSTEPModel preparation3D printing

My contribution

  • Prepared a chamber mockup assembly and a derived part file.
  • Separated the geometry into left and right exports, including a right-side variant without the end.

Deliverables

  • 3D printed PVD mockup.SLDASM
  • Native part and three sectional STEP exports

Recorded outcome

The available deliverable is a set of CAD and split geometry files suitable for reviewing the chamber layout and preparing a physical mockup.

Status & scope

The folder does not establish completion of the physical print or operation of the represented chamber.

  • 3D printed PVD mockup-part.SLDPRT
  • 3D printed PVD mockup right side.STEP

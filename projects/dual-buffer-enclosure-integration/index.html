MECHANICAL / CASE STUDY 07

Dual-buffer enclosure integration

CAD integration

Mechanical packaging for a laboratory dual bipolar buffer board. The enclosure assembly brings the existing PCB and connectors into a compact Hammond-style instrument box.

SolidWorksPCB packagingEnclosure integration

My contribution

  • Modeled the board and enclosure together to review the packaging and connector interfaces.
  • Retained the board’s source documentation alongside the mechanical integration files.

Deliverables

  • Boxed Bipolar Buffer.SLDASM
  • Board assembly and STEP model
  • Existing circuit documentation and revision notes

Recorded outcome

The saved assembly provides a mechanical integration reference for the buffer electronics. The underlying unity-gain circuit and its historical scope results are credited to the original lab design.

MECHANICAL / CASE STUDY 02

Alpha-detector stand

Built / documented

A breadboard-mounted fixture that holds an alpha detector and positions a filter-paper sample. Custom split clamps connect the detector to standard optical posts while a separate sheet-metal stand locates the sample.

SolidWorksWaterjetDrill pressTappingFixture design

My contribution

  • Designed the detector clamps, sample stand, and complete assembly.
  • Prepared waterjet drawings and adjusted the clamp fit after the first version.

Deliverables

  • SolidWorks parts and assembly
  • DXF drawing revisions and assembly/BOM notes

Recorded outcome

The build notes record a fit correction of 0.01 in on the clamp mating faces and distinguish the posts modeled in CAD from the 6 in posts used in the physical setup.

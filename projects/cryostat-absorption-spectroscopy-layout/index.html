OPTICS / CASE STUDY 15

Cryostat absorption-spectroscopy layout

CAD layout

An optical-breadboard layout for near-1115 nm absorption spectroscopy through a cryostat. The assembly integrates fiber-launch and receive packages, lenses, adjustable mounts, posts, and the cryostat’s mechanical envelope.

SolidWorksOptomechanicsFiber coupling1115 nmLayout planning

My contribution

  • Arranged the launch, focusing, sample, collection, and receive stations around a common beam axis.
  • Revised lens and support choices to clear the cryostat and documented the retained/suppressed design alternatives.

Deliverables

  • SolidWorks assembly revisions and beam-path SVG
  • V1b optical layout notes and component list
  • Saved placement, interference, and reopen logs

Recorded outcome

The V1b notes specify 200 mm singlets and a nominal lens-plane distance of 202.375 mm from the sample datum. The geometry provides a starting point for physical alignment.

1115 nmDesign wavelength
200 mmSelected lens focal length

Status & scope

The build notes identify alignment approximations. Fiber coupling, window properties, and optical performance still require experimental validation.

Credit & context

Commercial optics and cryostat models are manufacturer geometry. The contribution is assembly layout and integration.

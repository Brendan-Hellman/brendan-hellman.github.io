ELECTRONICS / CASE STUDY 17

Laser feedforward circuit

Circuit design / in progress

A feedforward circuit project connecting piezo-voltage changes to the laser-current modulation input. The saved schematic and notebook calculations address adjustable gain, polarity, buffering, and calibration of the required transfer function.

KiCadAnalog electronicsFeedforwardCalibrationTransfer functions

My contribution

  • Developed a KiCad schematic with an OPA4134-based signal chain, supply regulation, and named test points.
  • Derived the gain relationship from piezo tuning, driver response, the divider ratio, and potentiometer setting.

Deliverables

  • KiCad schematic, PCB work file, and custom footprint
  • Notebook equations and resistor-value/calibration notes

Recorded outcome

The archive captures a useful circuit-development stage: a detailed schematic and transfer-function calculation with the final calibration-dependent resistor value still open.

OPA4134Signal-chain component

Status & scope

The saved PCB is unfinished: native rendering reports a missing or malformed outline. No fabricated or validated feedforward board is claimed.

Credit & context

The project includes inherited lab circuit techniques and external laser-system references; this presentation focuses on the saved adaptation and calculations.

MECHANICAL / CASE STUDY 01

Source-region mounting systems

Built prototype + CAD

A modular support structure for the lab’s source-region oven, with a smaller prototype assembly and a larger frame design. The work connects equipment layout, custom flange supports, standard extrusion hardware, and a practical assembly sequence.

SolidWorks80/20 framingWaterjetDFMBOM

My contribution

  • Modeled the mounting assemblies and revised the support geometry to meet space constraints.
  • Developed slim CF600 flange clamps and modified 80/20 plates, with waterjet drawings and drilling/tapping details.

Deliverables

  • Native SolidWorks assemblies and STEP exports
  • Custom-part drawings, parts lists, and assembly instructions

Recorded outcome

The prototype mounting notes describe the oven installed on the frame and record a useful build revision: the second set of lower flange clamps was unnecessary in practice.

1/4 inPlate stock in build notes
1/2 inFlange-clamp stock

Status & scope

The installed prototype is documented in the assembly notes. The larger source-region frame is presented as a CAD design; no load-rating or structural certification is claimed.

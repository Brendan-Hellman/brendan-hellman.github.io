ELECTRONICS / CASE STUDY 18

Laser-diode protection PCB

Adapted schematic + PCB

A compact surface-mount implementation of a published laser-diode protection circuit. The KiCad project preserves the source reference and makes the external laser, driver, and cathode test-point connections explicit.

KiCadSMD layoutCircuit adaptationExternal wiring

My contribution

  • Recreated the thesis schematic and laid out a 50 by 40 mm PCB with labeled external pads.
  • Documented the off-board laser connection, driver connection, and component/package checks in the design.

Deliverables

  • KiCad schematic and routed PCB
  • Custom solder-pad footprint and symbol library

Recorded outcome

The design artifact translates a published protection network into a connector-free board layout that can be reviewed alongside its real-world wiring.

50 × 40 mmSaved board size

Status & scope

The design specifies generic SMD packages and calls for part-rating, pinout, and housing-bond verification. Fabrication and protection performance are not established.

Credit & context

Circuit source: Eric B. Norrgard thesis, Appendix G, Figure G.2, printed page 270; explicitly credited in the saved schematic.

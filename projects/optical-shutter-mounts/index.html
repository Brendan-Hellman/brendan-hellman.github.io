MECHANICAL / CASE STUDY 04

Optical shutter mounts

Fabrication documented

A compact, printable mount that holds a camera shutter on an optical post and provides space for a BNC connector. Multiple saved versions show the progression toward the final mount geometry.

SolidWorks3D printingPLAOptomechanics

My contribution

  • Iterated shutter-holder geometry through the saved v6-v10 revisions.
  • Integrated an 8-32 post interface, shutter slot, and side-connector cutout.

Deliverables

  • SolidWorks v10 part
  • 3MF print file, earlier STL variants, and installation guide

Recorded outcome

The final guide specifies basic PLA on a Bambu X1 Carbon and records the shutter’s installation orientation. The design packages the shutter as a reusable optical-table component.

Status & scope

Credit & context
